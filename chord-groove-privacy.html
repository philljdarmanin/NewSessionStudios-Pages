Chord Groove Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 May 2026

Chord Groove is a music practice app created by New Session Studios Pty Ltd.

Personal data

Chord Groove does not require an account and does not collect personal information from users.

Local app data

Chord Groove may store app settings, saved chord progressions, grooves, or practice-related preferences locally on your device. This information is used to make the app work and is not sent to New Session Studios.

Audio

Chord Groove plays audio so you can hear chords, grooves, and practice patterns. Speaker or audio output access is used only for playback.

Analytics and advertising

This starter policy assumes Chord Groove does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools. If these are added in future, this policy should be updated before release.

Children's privacy

Chord Groove is a general music practice tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions about Chord Groove, contact: support@newsessionstudios.com.au
